# 14 · Lessons from the fridge-hack build (Oct 2026, `motion` mode, 11:05 longform)

Measured on a real build. Most apply to **every** mode; motion-only details live in
`remotion-motion-explainer`, promo details in `video-promo-kit`.

## Voice (Sarvam clone)

- **Rate:** at `--pace 1.1` the clone spoke ≈160 wpm: 1,663 words → 10:29 of speech → 11:05
  of video with 0.6 s per scene tail. Budget ≈1,450 words for a 10-minute longform.
- **Cost:** 9,800 characters ≈ ₹21, 48 takes in ~3 minutes.
- **Whisper-check every take** against the script. `scripts/check-takes.py` (here) writes
  word-timestamp JSON and flags beats under 95% word match. Sarvam's own quality check passed a
  take that said "100" for "one hundred and eighty"; regenerating fixed it.
  - False alarms: ordinals ("11th" vs "eleventh"), homophones ("write"/"right"), and
    pronunciations that are actually right ("CERT-In" heard as "certain").
  - **After 2 failed takes, change the words**, not the seed: "per store" → "at each store";
    "the poisoned models" → "the models trained on poisoned text".
- **Respell for the voice only:** keep a map of text → spoken form ("E.Leclerc" → "E Leclair",
  "AIIMS" → "Aims", "CERT-In" → "Cert-In") applied when writing the TTS script. Captions keep
  the real spelling. Test the 2–3 beats with hard names before the full run.
- **Master the joined narration once**, as 48 kHz dual-mono stereo, then split it back per
  scene with 4 ms edge fades: one gain for the whole voice. −14.16 LUFS / −1.31 dBTP / LRA 2.5.

## Picture timing

- Scene length = its take + 0.6 s; animations use fractions of the scene, so a re-take moves
  nothing by hand.
- **Word cues:** a prop like `{"atWord": "fifty", "at": 0.5}` resolves to the moment that word is
  spoken. The fallback `at` is used before narration exists. This is the per-prop version of
  09-motion's "land on the word".
- **Captions:** use the script's words with Whisper's times (aligned by sequence matching), in
  ≤11-word phrases, the current word highlighted.

## Sound

- When no measure render is wanted, **each component fires its own effects at its event
  frames.** Same principle as 10-sound ("effects come from the picture"), with no detection
  pass.
- Synthesised effects + bed (seeded numpy). Bed at 0.45 in pauses, 0.25 under the voice,
  gliding over ±6 frames. Full mix measured −14.1 LUFS / −1.6 dBTP. Verify with an audio-only
  render (`--codec=wav`), which took 17 minutes for 11 minutes of video.

## Visual QA (any mode)

- One still per scene from a single bundle + contact sheets of 9, before any video render.
  Caught every overlap.
- Keep everything above the bottom 210 px when captions can wrap to two lines.
- **Never fade through black:** keep the ground under the transition layer. The owner read
  black frames as "no background".

## Gates and owner preferences

- Story first, with temporary captions; no TTS until the owner approves the script.
- No render, upload, schedule or post without explicit approval; ask when in doubt.
- The owner picks the thumbnail from ≥ 5 variants; educational framing; the subject is the
  largest text.
